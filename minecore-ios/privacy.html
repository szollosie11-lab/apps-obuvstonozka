MINECORE iOS — Privacy Policy

Version 1.0.0 · 18 October 2026

This policy applies to the iOS app built from MINECORE iOS DEV001. This version has no advertising, in-app purchases, account system or third-party analytics SDK.

Data stays on your device

Your game progress, active board, settings and game statistics are stored locally. A backup of the save is kept in the app’s private local storage. The app does not transmit gameplay data, identifiers, location, contacts or other personal data to the developer or advertising partners. It does not track you.

Deleting your progress

Use Settings → Reset progress to delete local progress and its local backup. Removing the app may also remove its local data. The app does not provide a cloud save service.

Support and external pages

If you choose to email support, the information you send is used to respond to your request. Do not send passwords or payment details. Any website you independently visit has its own privacy practices.
