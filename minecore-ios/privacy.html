MINECORE iOS — Privacy Policy

Updated 19 October 2026 · Unity advertising builds DEV002 and later

MINECORE stores your progress and settings locally. There is no game account, cloud save, subscription or in-app purchase.

Your advertising choice

Before starting Unity Ads, the app asks whether you want to enable contextual ads. You may choose to play without ads. Change this choice through Settings → Privacy options. Disabling ads stops further ad requests; close and reopen the app to end an already initialized SDK session. The previous DEV001 / build 3 contains no advertising SDK and sends no advertising data.

Unity Ads

If enabled, the native Unity Ads SDK provides occasional interstitial ads between completed campaign levels and optional rewarded ads. Ads are configured as non-behavioral, with personalized-ad consent declined and applicable data-sale/sharing opt-out enabled. This app does not request App Tracking Transparency authorization or supply a custom player identifier. Unity may show its own privacy notice where required. TestFlight and debug builds request test ads.

Unity's published SDK disclosures cover approximate country-level location derived from IP, device and SDK identifiers, app/ad interactions, advertising and other usage data, performance measurements and technical device information. Unity also discloses purchase-related data, user identifiers, and optional support information when you report an ad. We do not send your saved board, campaign progress, contacts, photos or precise GPS location to Unity. The app does not ask for location permission.

Data may be processed by Unity and its advertising service providers to deliver ads, measure performance, maintain service security and prevent fraud. Retention, international transfers and privacy requests are described in Unity's privacy policy. Unity's game player and app user privacy policy provides additional details.

Progress and deletion

Settings → Reset progress removes local gameplay data and its local backup. Advertising preferences are separate. Deleting the app removes its local storage; request deletion of information held by Unity through Unity's privacy channels. No precise location, contact list or payment details are requested by MINECORE.

Slovensky

Postup a nastavenia hry zostávajú v zariadení. Pred spustením Unity Ads si môžete zvoliť kontextové reklamy alebo hranie bez reklám. Voľbu zmeníte cez Nastavenia → Možnosti súkromia. Vypnutie zastaví ďalšie reklamné požiadavky; pre ukončenie už spusteného SDK aplikáciu zatvorte a znovu otvorte.

Unity môže spracovať približnú polohu odvodenú z IP, identifikátory zariadenia a SDK, údaje o reklamách, používaní, výkone a technické údaje. Dokumentácia Unity uvádza aj údaje o nákupoch a dobrovoľné hlásenia problémov s reklamou. Neodosielame uloženú hru, kontakty, fotografie ani presnú GPS polohu. Reklamy sú nastavené ako nepersonalizované; aplikácia nežiada povolenie na sledovanie. TestFlight používa testovacie reklamy. Podrobnosti a práva k údajom nájdete v prepojených zásadách Unity. Kontakt: info@obuvstonozka.eu.
