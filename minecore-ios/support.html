How to play

Tap a covered cell to reveal it. Numbers show the number of adjacent mines. Long-press to flag a suspected mine, or use Flag mode. Complete the displayed objective. The in-game Help and Field Manual explain each mechanic as it is introduced.

Progress and offline play

The game works offline and saves progress on this device. The app does not provide cross-device synchronization. Avoid deleting the app if you want to retain your progress. This iOS version has no ads or in-app purchases.
