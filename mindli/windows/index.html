Before you play

Can I play MINDli Quiz offline?

Yes. Bundled quizzes and local minigames can be played offline. Optional question updates and feedback features use an internet connection.

Does the Windows version contain ads?

No. The Windows edition is a one-time purchase with no ads or in-app purchases. Pricing and monetisation can differ on other platforms.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required to start. Your local profile and progress are saved on your device.

Does this version include online multiplayer?

This Windows release focuses on local quizzes and minigames. Online matchmaking and native Wi-Fi/QR multiplayer are not configured in this edition.

Where can I download the game safely?

Use the official MINDli Quiz Microsoft Store page. Check its current system requirements and price before purchasing.
