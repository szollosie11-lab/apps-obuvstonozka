Jinx Market is operated by Mgr. Erik Szöllösi, trading as TORY MINDli. For support and privacy requests contact tory.mindli01@gmail.com.

Local gameplay data

Saved matches, hands, scores, language, sound settings and advertising choices are stored on your device. The game has no registration, gameplay account, gameplay server or online player chat. We do not receive your local match history. Application backups are disabled. Clear the application's storage or uninstall it to delete local game data.

Unity Ads

The app uses the Unity Ads SDK to show advertising between selected completed matches. Unity may collect and share approximate location (for example inferred from an IP address), device and advertising identifiers, personal identifiers assigned by the SDK, diagnostic information and advertising views, taps and interaction data. Unity's SDK disclosure also describes purchase-history and app-activity processing where applicable to its advertising services. Jinx Market does not offer in-app purchases or send a purchase history of its own.

Unity uses this information for advertising delivery and marketing, analytics, app functionality, fraud prevention, security and compliance. Data is encrypted in transit. Advertising processing may occur outside your country. Retention, recipients, legal grounds, international-transfer safeguards and access/deletion rights are explained in Unity's privacy policy and Unity Ads data disclosures.

Your choices and rights

The advertising SDK is not initialized until an advertising choice has been recorded. Personalized and contextual advertising choices are available through Settings → Privacy & ads. Contextual advertising declines personalization but still involves the SDK data processing needed to operate advertising. Selecting “Not now” leaves the SDK uninitialized until you make a choice. Change your choice later in Settings. Changing a choice does not erase data already processed.

For Unity-held data, use the privacy options in a Unity advertisement or follow the rights and deletion instructions in Unity's privacy policy. You can contact us for assistance. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion, object to processing, restrict processing, withdraw consent and complain to your data protection authority. We can delete local game data only through the device controls described above because we do not hold it on a server.

Audience and permissions

The app is intended for players aged 16 and over and is not directed primarily to children. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location permissions. It contains no real-money gambling or cash prizes.

Changes

This page will be updated when the application's data practices change. The effective date is shown above.
