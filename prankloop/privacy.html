PRANKLOOP: Little Chaos — Privacy Policy

Developer: Erik Szöllösi / TORY MINDli
Android package: eu.obuvstonozka.prankloop
Updated: 21 November 2026

Offline gameplay and local storage

PRANKLOOP is an offline prank-room game for ages 13 and older. It does not require registration, a game account or a gameplay server. Language, audio preferences, advertising choice, stars, best score and discoveries are stored on your device. The game does not include a separate gameplay analytics service or collect your name, email, messages or files.

Optional advertising

The Android app integrates Unity Ads. The advertising SDK is not initialized until you choose to allow ads. You may play without ads. Ads may appear between completed challenge rounds, never as a prerequisite to completing a challenge. The app requests non-behavioral advertising, rejects personalized-ad consent and enables the SDK opt-out setting.

Even limited-personalization ads can involve device and advertising identifiers, network-derived approximate location, ad interactions and SDK diagnostics. Unity and its partners process these data for ad delivery, measurement, service operation and fraud prevention. SDK data is encrypted in transit. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for processing, international transfers, retention and your rights. The game does not request precise location, camera, microphone or contacts access.

Your choices and deletion

Settings → Advertising lets you disable future ad requests. If you already enabled advertising, close and reopen the app after disabling it to ensure the SDK is not initialized in the new session. You may also manage advertising identifiers in Android settings. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove local progress and preferences. There is no developer game account to delete. For Unity-held data, use Unity's privacy request and deletion routes or contact us for assistance.
