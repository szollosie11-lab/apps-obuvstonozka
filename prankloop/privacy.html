PRANKLOOP: Little Chaos — Privacy Policy

Developer: Erik Szöllösi / TORY MINDli
Android package: eu.obuvstonozka.prankloop
Updated: 26 November 2026

Offline gameplay and local storage

PRANKLOOP is an offline prank-room game for ages 13 and older. It does not require registration, a game account or a gameplay server. Language, audio preferences, stars, best score and discoveries are stored on your device. Toy-shop token balance and unlocked toys are also stored locally and are removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. The game does not include a separate gameplay analytics service or collect your name, email, messages or files.

Advertising

Android version 0.5.1 (12) and later integrates Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) alongside Unity Ads. Earlier Android versions use Unity Ads. AdMob is the first choice for the existing interstitial slot, with a ready Unity ad as fallback; adding AdMob does not double the ad frequency. This free game is supported by ads and has no in-game switch to disable advertising. After the first-use privacy notice, the SDK may initialize to request contextual ads. Interstitial ads may appear after every five completed rounds or natural play breaks, never as a prerequisite to completing a challenge. Version 0.7.0 (14) also offers optional Unity rewarded videos in the toy shop. A fully completed video grants 25 local tokens; skipped or failed videos do not grant tokens. Version 0.8.0 (15) also grants five local tokens once for each completed mini challenge and new discovery; these gameplay milestones are stored locally to prevent duplicate rewards. Tokens have no cash value. The app supplies 20 starter tokens and offers permanent local toy unlocks. No real-money purchase is used for these unlocks. The app requests non-personalized Google ads with conservative under-age-of-consent treatment and maximum ad content rating T. UMP checks whether ads may be requested. Unity uses non-behavioral advertising, rejects personalized-ad consent and enables the SDK opt-out setting.

Even limited-personalization ads can involve device and advertising identifiers, network-derived approximate location, ad interactions and SDK diagnostics. Google, Unity and their partners process these data for ad delivery, measurement, service operation and fraud prevention. SDK data is encrypted in transit. See Google's Privacy Policy, Google advertising information and Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for processing, international transfers, retention and your rights. The game does not request precise location, camera, microphone or contacts access.

Your choices and deletion

There is no in-game ad-disable option. This does not remove your privacy rights: use the Google ad privacy options in the game's privacy screen when required, Unity’s privacy controls or the privacy icon on ads to manage vendor choices and request access or deletion. You may also manage advertising identifiers in Android settings. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove local progress and preferences. There is no developer game account to delete. For Google- or Unity-held data, use the respective provider's privacy request and deletion routes or contact us for assistance.
