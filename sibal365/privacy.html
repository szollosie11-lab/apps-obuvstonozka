SIBAL 365 — Privacy

Effective 17 November 2026. Developer: TORY MINDli.

Local game data

Your progress, preferences, nickname and optional written answers are stored on your device. The developer does not receive these game records. The game has no account system or game server. You can clear collected progress in Settings. Removing the app removes its local data. Drawings in the first development version are temporary and are not uploaded.

Optional advertising

The Android version integrates Unity Ads. Advertising is optional and disabled until the player enables it. The game uses contextual/non-behavioral ad configuration. Unity may still process device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, diagnostic information and ad interactions for ad delivery, measurement, security and other purposes described in its own policy. You can disable ads in the game settings. This prevents further game requests for ads; it does not erase records already held by Unity.

Read Unity’s Privacy Policy and its information about privacy choices and data requests.

Sharing with friends

Sharing opens a system share sheet. You choose the receiving app and recipient and send the message yourself. The selected app operates under its own terms and privacy policy. SIBAL 365 does not automatically send emails, read your address book or verify another player’s completion. The share text includes the card, challenge, nickname if provided, and the player’s self-reported completion.

Permissions and audience

The game itself does not request camera, contacts, microphone or location access. The Android ad integration uses internet and network-state access. This is a general-audience creativity game planned for players aged 13 and older, not a service directed primarily to children.
