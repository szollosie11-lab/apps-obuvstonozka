SIBAL 365 — Privacy

Updated 18 November 2026. Developer: TORY MINDli.

Local game data

Your progress, completion dates, preferences, nickname, optional written answers and drawings are stored on your device. The developer does not receive these game records. The game has no account system or game server. You can clear game progress in Settings. Removing the app removes its local data.

Optional advertising

The Android version integrates Unity Ads. Advertising is optional and disabled until the player enables it. The game uses contextual/non-behavioral ad configuration. Unity may still process device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, diagnostic information and ad interactions for ad delivery, measurement, security and other purposes described in its own policy. You can disable ads in the game settings. This prevents further game requests for ads; it does not erase records already held by Unity.

Read Unity’s Privacy Policy for privacy choices and data requests.

Sharing and saving pictures

Sharing prepares an illustrated card with the task, card number and a notice that completion is self-reported. Its accompanying caption may include your nickname if provided. Your private written answer and drawing are not included automatically. You choose the receiving app and recipient and send the content yourself. The selected app operates under its own privacy policy. SIBAL 365 does not automatically post, send messages, read your address book or verify completion. You can instead save a PNG through the Android document picker. Shared images are temporarily cached on your device with access granted only for the selected sharing operation. Copying a caption places it on your device clipboard only when you request it.

Optional local reminders

Reminders are off by default. If you enable them, the selected local time and language are stored on your device. On Android 13 and later, notification permission is requested only when enabling reminders. You can turn reminders off in the game or Android settings. Delivery is inexact and may be delayed by battery restrictions. No push-notification service, notification token or reminder schedule is sent to the developer.

Permissions and audience

The game does not request camera, contacts, microphone or location access. The ad integration uses internet, network-state and advertising-identifier permissions. Optional reminders use notification permission and reschedule after a restart or time-zone change. Saving a picture uses the system document picker, without broad storage access. This is a general-audience creativity game for players aged 13 and older, not a service directed primarily to children.
