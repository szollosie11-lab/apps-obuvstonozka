SIBAL365 Privacy

Updated 23 November 2026. Developer: TORY MINDli. Shared online features were introduced in version 0.4.0. The AdMob section describes the optional integration prepared for version 0.4.1; it applies only to installed versions that include it.

Local progress and photos

Solo progress, your collection, private notes, drawings and selected photos stay on this phone. Photos are stored privately by the app and are never uploaded to the game service. Imported images are resized and re-encoded without their original image metadata. Remove individual photos beside their challenge; resetting local progress also removes local photos. Uninstalling removes local app data.

Optional shared challenges

When you choose an online group, the app sends the card ID, language, acceptance and completion times, optional nickname and a random installation key over HTTPS. The service stores a hash of the key to associate results and support deletion. No email, password, contacts or location is required. Photos, drawings and private notes are not sent to the game service. Nicknames and times are visible to people with the invitation; share the link only with your intended group.

The dedicated service uses Google Cloud Run and private Cloud Storage in europe-west3. Invitations can be accepted for seven days. Group records then remain available only as needed to finish and view results; storage objects are automatically deleted after 30 days without another write. Google may process network and operational logs including IP addresses. Use Delete my online data in Settings while connected to remove your online group data. Deleting a group you created also cancels its invitation. Resetting or uninstalling alone does not delete server records; use the online deletion option before uninstalling.

Sharing and self-reported results

The system share sheet lets you choose an app and recipient and confirm sending. Nothing is posted automatically. Nickname, date, private note and photo are excluded from shared images until you explicitly select them in the preview. Copying a code uses the clipboard only when requested. Your selected social app processes the shared content under its own policy. Completion is based on trust: the service measures time but does not verify that the real-world task was performed.

Backups and reminders

A manual JSON backup may contain your nickname, private notes, drawings, challenge codes and history. It does not contain photos or the installation key. Keep backups private; they are not uploaded automatically. Reminders are local and optional.

Optional advertising

Unity Ads remains optional. If enabled, Unity may process device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, diagnostics and ad interactions under its own policy. Disable ads in Settings to stop further game ad requests; this does not erase records Unity already holds.

Optional Google AdMob (version 0.4.1)

Google AdMob is a separate option, off by default, for players aged 16 or older. The app checks Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) privacy choices before initializing the ads SDK and requesting an ad. Enabling the option can contact Google for the privacy message, even if no ad is ultimately shown. We request non-personalized ads; this does not mean that no personal data is processed. Google Mobile Ads can collect and share IP addresses (including approximate location derived from them), device and account identifiers such as advertising ID and app set ID, ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Transfers use encrypted connections. The app does not supply your game progress, nickname, photos or private notes as ad targeting parameters.

Turn off Google AdMob in Settings to stop further game ad requests. If UMP requires a privacy-options entry, Google ad privacy choices is available in Settings and Privacy so you can change or withdraw choices. Native advertising preferences are kept on this phone and are excluded from game progress backups. Disabling ads does not delete information Google has already processed; use Google privacy controls and policy contacts for those requests. Unity remains separately optional with its existing privacy settings. Across both providers, fullscreen ads use one shared limit: at least five completed practice or duel tasks and five minutes between ad attempts, only at a natural break, never during an active task.

Permissions and audience

This is a general-audience creativity game for players aged 13 and older, not a service directed primarily to children. The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location access. Selected photos and exports use system document pickers without broad media or storage permissions. Internet, network-state and advertising-identifier permissions support the optional features described above; optional reminders use notification permission.
