SABOTAGE! Bistro Duel — Privacy Policy

Developer: Erik Szöllösi / TORY MINDli
Android package: com.tory.sabotage
Last updated: 18 November 2026

How the game works

The game does not require registration or an account. Solo and same-device multiplayer gameplay run locally. Language, sound settings, completed matches, best scores and cosmetic progress are stored on your device. The developer does not operate a gameplay account server for this game.

Advertising

The Android app integrates Unity Ads, without Google AdMob. Ads can appear between matches. Optional rewarded ads unlock cosmetic items. The app does not offer an ad-removal purchase.

Unity and its advertising partners may process device or advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from network information, device and app characteristics, ad views and interactions, performance and diagnostic information. These data support advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. Processing and retention are described in Unity's Privacy Policy and Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Your choices

Use Settings → Advertising choices to select personalized or contextual advertising. Contextual advertising can still involve technical data needed to deliver and measure ads. You can also manage advertising identifiers and privacy controls in Android settings. Advertising availability depends on the network, region and provider.

Sharing results

Sharing is optional and opens Android's share chooser. Only the result text and game link selected for sharing are passed to the destination app you choose. That app's privacy policy applies. The game does not access your social account credentials or post automatically.

Storage and deletion

You can delete local game progress by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling it. There is no developer account to delete. For data processed by Unity, use the privacy choices and request routes in Unity's privacy policy or contact us for assistance.

Security and audience

Network requests use the advertising SDK's transport; the app disallows cleartext traffic. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise GPS location access. It is a general-audience competitive game and is not designed specifically for children. Local two-player mode uses the same screen.

Contact and changes

Questions or privacy requests: tory.mindli01@gmail.com. Include the game name and request details; do not send passwords or payment details. This page may be updated when the game or its data practices change.
