Local game data

Your scores, language, sound, vibration, tutorial progress, advertising choice and completed-game counter are stored on your device. Android 1.1 and 1.2 additionally store local chef-order and weekly-tasting stars, daily-task progress, collected tastes, unlocked hats and kitchen decorations, badges, volume and accessibility settings and a challenge code. Weekly stars reset locally on Monday; other earned collections remain. These records are not uploaded to a HOPLA server. If you use Share challenge, Android opens its share sheet with the challenge code and, when sharing a result, your score; you choose whether and where to send that text. No account, social login, cloud save, subscription or in-app purchase is required. The app does not request contacts, photos, microphone, camera, precise location or payment details. Uninstalling the app or clearing its app data removes local data. The game is available offline; ads require a connection.

Your advertising choice

Before initializing Unity Ads, HOPLA asks whether to enable contextual ads. You can decline and continue playing. Change your choice in Settings → Privacy & ads. Disabling ads stops further requests by the game; to end an already initialized SDK session, fully close and reopen the app. No personalized-ad consent is inferred from continuing to play. Training and the tutorial are ad-free.

Unity Ads

If enabled, an interstitial ad may be displayed at a natural break after every two completed competitive games, when leaving results. Android 1.1 and 1.2 include Classic, Not edible, chef orders, two-player dinner, memory, hungry guest, minute feast and friend challenges in this counter; Android 1.2 also counts completed weekly tasting challenges. On iOS 1.0.1 the counter covers Classic and Not edible. Practice, focused training, tutorials and abandoned runs do not count. Ads do not interrupt an active round. If an ad is unavailable, play continues. Release builds request live Unity inventory; debug builds request test inventory.

Unity Ads may process IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and SDK identifiers, technical device information, ad/app interactions, usage and advertising data, diagnostics and performance information. Unity's SDK disclosures also describe purchase-related measurements and information you voluntarily provide when reporting an ad. HOPLA has no purchase feature and sends no saved game state or custom player identifier to Unity.

Ads are configured as contextual/non-behavioral, with personalization consent declined and privacy opt-out signals applied before SDK initialization. The Android app removes the advertising-ID permission. The iOS app does not request App Tracking Transparency authorization or IDFA access. These measures do not mean the advertising SDK processes no technical or device data.

Unity and its service providers may use data for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service security, and may process it outside your country. Provider retention periods, international-transfer safeguards and access/deletion requests are described in Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. HOPLA does not operate a separate analytics server or sell a player database.

Support and your rights

If you email us, we use the information you voluntarily provide to respond to your request. Do not send passwords or payment details. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent and complain to a data-protection authority. Contact the operator about HOPLA data and Unity through its privacy channels about data held by Unity. HOPLA is designed for a general audience aged 13 and above and is not directed to children. Material policy changes will be reflected here.
